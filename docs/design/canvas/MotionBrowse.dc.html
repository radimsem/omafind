5 Subvolumes and snapshots{{pPage}}
5.3 Working with snapshots
Btrfs snapshots are copy-on-write clones of a subvolume. A snapshot is created instantly and takes no extra space until either side changes.
Creating a snapshot
btrfs subvolume snapshot -r /home /snap/home-0412 Add -r to make the snapshot read-only. A read-only snapshot can be sent to another disk with btrfs send.
Rolling back
To roll back, move the broken subvolume aside and take a writable snapshot of the read-only copy in its place. Reboot when the root subvolume is affected.
Deleting old snapshots
Deleting a snapshot frees only the extents no other subvolume references.
